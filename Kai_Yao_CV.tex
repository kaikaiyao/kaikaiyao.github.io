\section*{Research Profile}
I am an AI researcher specializing in secure and trustworthy machine learning, generative-model provenance, post-deployment model auditing, and ML systems. My doctoral work integrates statistical theory, adaptive threat modelling, and reproducible large-scale benchmarking to characterize when provenance and attribution methods remain reliable under realistic editing and deployment changes, alongside work on privacy-induced unfairness and AI governance. Prior full-time industry engineering at Intel and Huawei and scientific-imaging research at Johns Hopkins ground this work in deployable systems.

\section*{Research Experience}

\role{University of Edinburgh}{PhD Researcher, School of Informatics}{Edinburgh, UK}{May 2023 – Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Post-Deployment Auditing \& Statistical Limits (NeurIPS 2026):} Developed \textit{FARE}, an output-only black-box audit learning patch-level acceptance regions from certified generator outputs to flag model, checkpoint, and pipeline substitutions (\textbf{92.6\%–99.5\%} mean TPR at \textbf{1\%} FPR; \textbf{92.4\%–99.4\%} under white-box PGD attacks). Established minimax total-variation limits on passive provenance under editing and analyzed verifier-interface leakage enabling surrogate evasion (\textit{Can Pixels Alone?}).
  \item \textbf{Adversarial Evaluation of Fingerprints \& Local Watermarks (IEEE SaTML 2026 Oral; ACM AISec 2026):} Formalized white-box and black-box removal and forgery threat models, benchmarking \textbf{14 fingerprinting methods across 12 GAN/VAE/diffusion generators} under 5 attack strategies; co-developed a \textbf{55-transformation benchmark} decoupling spatial localization from payload recovery for local invisible image watermarks.
  \item \textbf{Keyed Attribution, Fairness \& AI Governance (Preprint; SaTML 2025 Oral; AIES 2026):} Designed \textit{SPRINT}, a keyed passive-attribution method using verifier-held pixel-reconstruction targets (\textbf{98.83\%} clean accuracy; \textbf{<1\%} adaptive attack success with private keys); systematized 20 differential-privacy fairness studies into a four-layer taxonomy; formulated a capability framework and audited 30 university AI policies across four open-weight LLMs.
\end{itemize}
